# AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) & Credibility Instructions

These rules MUST be followed whenever adding new content, services, or case studies to the site to ensure maximum visibility on AI answer engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Bing.

## 1. Direct Answer Blocks
- Always start pages or major sections with a clear, self-contained paragraph (2-3 sentences).
- Answer "What is this and who is it for?" immediately.
- Use simple, jargon-free language.

## 2. Question-Based Headers
- Use H2/H3 headers that mirror actual user questions.
- Example: "How does an AI voice agent help a restaurant?" instead of "Our AI Voice Service".

## 3. Credibility Signals (MUST BE INCLUDED)
- **Byline:** Every service description or article must have a byline.
  - Format: "Written by Sai Videsh, AI automation consultant — [Experience] building AI systems for Bengaluru [Industries]. Last updated: [Month Year]."
- **Attribution:** Case studies must cite specific business names, locations, and measurable results.
  - Format: "[Business Name] ([Area]) saw a [X%] increase in [Metric] within [Timeframe]."
- **Trust Anchors:** Maintain a clear "About this agency" section with location, specialization, and founding details (Founded 2026).

## 4. FAQ Harvesting
- Maintain a dedicated FAQ section with questions written from the user's perspective.
- Answers should be direct and factual (3-4 sentences).

---
*Note: AI models prioritize content with clear human authorship and verifiable local data.*
